Safety Products

Gloves, glasses, masks, hearing protection, knee pads, hi-vis and site barricade gear.

Surrey Hills Garden Supplies carries the safety gear a tradesperson or home owner needs before starting a job: leather rigger, neoprene, PVC gauntlet and general purpose gloves, safety glasses, disposable dust masks, corded and uncorded ear plugs, heavy-duty knee pads and kneeling boards, hi-vis vests compliant to AS/NZS 4602:1999, and site control items including 75mm barrier tape, 1150mm reflective bollards and witches hats. Our staff will talk through what you need to stay protected on your next project.
