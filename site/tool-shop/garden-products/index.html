Garden Products

Fertilisers, manures, lime, gypsum, lawn seed, weed mat, geotextile and pest control.

Garden products are the bags and bottles that make the soil and mulch work harder: Dynamic Lifter pelletised fertiliser, 25-litre bags of poultry, sheep and cow manure, gypsum for clay soils, garden lime, lawn starter and repair kits, Valley Seeds and budget lawn seed, weed mat and geotextile, Charlie Carp fish fertiliser and Confidor for pests. We also keep star pickets, treated pine sleepers, pavers and plastic edging on the same aisle. All available at the Surrey Hills yard or added to a delivery.

Garden Products questions

Which manure is best for vegetables?

Poultry manure is the richest all-purpose organic fertiliser; cow and sheep manure are gentler soil conditioners. Many gardeners use a mix of both.
