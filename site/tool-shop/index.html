Tool Shop in Surrey Hills

Tool Shop for trade and home gardeners

The tool shop at Surrey Hills Garden Supplies is stocked for the trades that use our materials: bricklayers, concreters, plasterers, tilers, landscapers and carpenters. Trowels from Marshalltown and Marco Pesaro, floats, hawks, jointers, levels, line blocks and pins, diamond blades, shovels, rakes, brooms, wheelbarrows and the hammers, chisels and bolt cutters that go with them. The safety range covers gloves, glasses, dust masks, ear protection, knee pads, hi-vis vests, barricade tape, witches hats and bollards. Garden products include fertilisers, manures, lime, gypsum, lawn seed, weed mat, geotextile and pest control, and we keep a few odd items such as half wine barrels. Everything is on the shelf at the Canterbury Road yard, so you can pick up a bag of cement, a trowel and a pair of gloves in one visit. We aim for trade quality at affordable prices, and if you need something we do not have on the shelf, ask and we will try to get it in.

How to choose

Which tool shop product do I need?

Bricklaying

Brick trowels, jointers, line blocks and pins, brick carriers, bolsters and a masonry splash brush. See the tools page.

Concreting

Bull floats, edgers, magnesium and wood floats, kneeling boards, placer rakes and mixing paddles.

Safety on site

Gloves, safety glasses, dust masks, ear muffs and plugs, knee pads, hi-vis vests and site barricade gear.

Lawn and garden care

Lawn seed, starter and repair products, fertilisers, manures, lime and gypsum on the garden products page.

Good to know

Tool Shop questions

Do you sell tools to the public?

Yes. The tool shop serves trades and the general public at the same prices.

Which trowel brands do you carry?

Marshalltown and Marco Pesaro trowels and floats, plus Ancora Pavan stainless pool trowels, among others listed on the tools page.

Do you stock safety gear?

Yes. Gloves, glasses, masks, hearing protection, knee pads, vests and barricade products are on the safety products page.

Can I order tools for delivery?

Tools and garden products can be added to a delivery of bulk materials. Call the yard to arrange it.
