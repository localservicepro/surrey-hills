Tools

Trowels, floats, jointers, levels, blades, hammers, rakes and brooms for brickies, concreters and landscapers.

The tool wall at Surrey Hills Garden Supplies is stocked for the trades that buy our sand, cement and stone. Bricklayers will find Marshalltown and Marco Pro brick trowels, jointers, line blocks and pins, brick carriers and bolsters. Concreters get bull floats, edgers, magnesium and wood floats, kneeling boards and placer rakes. Everyone else finds diamond blades, levels, sledge and claw hammers, cold chisels, post hole shovels, landscape rakes and brooms. The full list is below; call if you need something not on it.
