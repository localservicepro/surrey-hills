Drainage in Surrey Hills

Drainage for trade and home gardeners

Water that has nowhere to go ruins lawns, undermines paving and pushes retaining walls over. Surrey Hills Garden Supplies stocks the drainage supplies to move it: agricultural (ag) pipe in a range of sizes, plastic drainage pits, grates and fittings, geotextile fabric to keep silt out, and the stone that goes around it all, with scoria and 14mm screenings held in bays on the Canterbury Road yard. Plumbers, landscapers and home owners all buy the same kit for the same jobs: a trench behind a retaining wall, a rubble drain along a boggy fence line, or a soakage pit for a downpipe. Large and small orders are accepted, from a single length of pipe to a bulk load of scoria delivered on our truck. In Melbourne's eastern suburbs we aim to deliver in less than two hours. If you are not sure what size pipe or how much stone a trench needs, tell us the length and depth and we will work it out with you.

How to choose

Which drainage product do I need?

Behind retaining walls

Slotted ag pipe in a sock at the base of the wall, backfilled with scoria, with geotextile between the scoria and the soil.

Soggy lawns and fence lines

A rubble drain: trench, geotextile, ag pipe and 14mm screenings or scoria, finished with soil and turf.

Downpipes and surface water

Solid pipe to a pit or soakage well filled with screenings.

Good to know

Drainage questions

What stone goes around ag pipe?

Scoria or 14mm screenings. Both are free draining with no fines. Scoria is lighter and the usual choice behind walls; screenings are the usual choice in trenches.

What sizes of ag pipe do you stock?

Several sizes of agricultural pipe plus plastic fittings. Call the yard with the job and we will confirm what is on the shelf.

How much scoria do I need for a trench?

Multiply trench length by width by depth in metres for cubic metres, then use the scoria calculator to convert to tonnes or bags.

Do you deliver small drainage orders?

Yes. Large and small orders are accepted, and pipe and fittings can ride along with a bulk delivery of stone.
