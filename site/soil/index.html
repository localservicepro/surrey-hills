Soil for trade and home gardeners

Good soil is the difference between a garden that struggles and one that takes off. Surrey Hills Garden Supplies stocks three soils that cover almost every job in a Melbourne garden: a blended soil for lawns, instant turf and general garden beds, a premium organic soil for vegetable patches and planter boxes, and a sandier native garden mix for Australian natives that need sharp drainage. How to choose

Which soil product do I need?

Lawns and instant turf

Blended soil is the ideal base under instant turf and for levelling or top dressing lawns. Lay 100mm, rake level and firm before turfing.

Vegie patches, raised beds and planter boxes

Premium organic soil is a composted, organic mix with the nutrients vegetables and flowers need. Fill the bed and plant straight into it.

Australian natives

Native garden mix is a sandier, low-nutrient version of blended soil. Natives such as grevilleas and banksias prefer it because it drains freely.

Not sure?

Call the yard and tell us what you are planting. We will point you to the right soil and the right quantity.

Good to know

Soil questions

How much does a cubic metre of soil weigh?

Garden soil weighs roughly 1.2 tonnes per cubic metre when it is dry and more when wet. That matters for trailers: a standard 6x4 trailer carries about half a cubic metre.

How much soil do I need for a new lawn?

Allow 100mm of blended soil under instant turf. One cubic metre covers about 10 square metres at that depth. Use the calculator on the blended soil page.

Can I pick up soil in bags?

Yes. Every soil can be bagged into 15kg bags at the yard. For more than a few bags, a trailer or a bulk delivery is better value.

What soil should I use for vegetables?

Premium organic soil. It is a composted organic mix made for vegetable gardens and planter boxes. Our blog post on soil for vegetable gardens goes into more detail.

Do you deliver soil on weekends?

Delivery days and times depend on the truck schedule. Call (03) 9890 3901 and we will give you the next available slot for your suburb.
