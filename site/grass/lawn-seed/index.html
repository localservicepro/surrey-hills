Lawn Seed

Seed blends from 1kg to 25kg bags, plus lawn starter and lawn repair products.

Seeding is the economical way to start or thicken a lawn, and Surrey Hills Garden Supplies carries seed in sizes from a 1kg bag for patching to 25kg bags for a whole block. The range includes budget lawn blends, drought-tolerant blends for Melbourne summers, Valley Seeds varieties, and the lawn starter and lawn repair products that give new seed the best chance. Sow onto raked blended soil, keep it moist for the first few weeks, and feed with a starter fertiliser.

Good to know

Lawn Seed questions

How much lawn seed do I need?

Sowing rates vary by blend; as a guide most lawn blends go down at 25–35 grams per square metre for a new lawn. Check the bag and we will help you size it.

When should I sow lawn seed in Melbourne?

Autumn and spring, when the soil is warm and rain is likely. Avoid the middle of summer unless you can water daily.
