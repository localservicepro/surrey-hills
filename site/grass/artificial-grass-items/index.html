Artificial Grass Installation Items

Joining tape, 150mm nails, glue, rubber infill, Smell B Gone and kiln dried sand for synthetic lawns.

A synthetic lawn is only as good as what holds it down and fills it. Surrey Hills Garden Supplies carries the full installation kit: 150mm (6-inch) metal nails to secure the edges, joining tape in 5m and 20m rolls, glue and wet gel for seams, rubber infill, and Smell B Gone, an Australian-made organic infill in 15kg bags that neutralises pet odours and keeps the grass cooler in summer. Pair it with crusher dust for the base and kiln dried sand for the infill, both stocked on the yard.

About artificial grass installation items
