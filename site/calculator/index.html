How the calculator works

Volume is length × width × depth (or π × radius² × depth for a circle), converted from millimetres of depth to metres. The result is rounded up to the nearest 0.25 m³ because that is how we load and price bulk product. Weight is the raw volume multiplied by the product's typical density in tonnes per cubic metre, and bags are the weight divided by the bag size, rounded up. Tick the 10% wastage box for beds that are uneven or for materials that compact, such as crushed rock and crusher dust.

Coverage rule of thumb

One cubic metre covers about 10 m² at 100mm, 13 m² at 75mm, 20 m² at 50mm and 6.5 m² at 150mm. A 6x4 trailer holds roughly half a cubic metre.
