Garden Supplies Delivered to Glen Iris

Glen Iris is about fifteen minutes south-west of the yard, down through Camberwell along Burke Road or Warrigal Road. We deliver bulk mulch, soil, sand and stone to Glen Iris by the cubic metre, and the yard is an easy trip for bags or a trailer load.

Glen Iris runs along Gardiners Creek, and the creek is the thing to understand about its gardens. The land slopes down to the creek from both sides, the soil is heavy clay with patches of fill, and the low streets near Ferndale Park and the Gardiners Creek Trail can get waterlogged after a big rain. The houses are mostly interwar brick and weatherboard on decent blocks, with a growing number of renovated and rebuilt homes along the High Street end.

Drainage comes up in nearly every Glen Iris conversation. An ag drain down the side of the house or behind a retaining wall needs scoria to backfill the trench and the pipe, pits and geotextile from our drainage supplies. Where the problem is the clay itself, organic mulch forked through the beds opens it up over time, and raised beds built above the clay get filled with premium organic soil. For a soakage pit or a concrete pour, 14mm screenings go on the same load.

Family gardens

Glen Iris is a family suburb and the backyards show it: a lawn for the kids, a vegie patch, a trampoline, and a sandpit, which is why triple washed sand is a regular small order here. Lawns get patched with blended soil and seed in autumn, and beds are mulched with pine bark in spring. These are mostly trailer-sized jobs, and a lot of Glen Iris customers do exactly that, driving up to the yard on a Saturday morning and getting loaded.

For a bigger job, bulk delivery by the cubic metre is tipped on the driveway or nature strip. The streets are mostly wide with good access, but the blocks that back onto the creek can have steep driveways, so mention that when you order. 15kg bags cover anything smaller and can be collected any time.

Ring the yard on (03) 9890 3901 with the Glen Iris address, the number of metres and what the job is, and we will get it sorted.
