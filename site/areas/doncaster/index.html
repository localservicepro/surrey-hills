Garden Supplies Delivered to Doncaster

Doncaster is about fifteen minutes north of the yard, up Elgar Road or Station Street and across the Koonung Creek. We deliver bulk garden and building supplies to Doncaster and Doncaster East by the cubic metre, with trailer loading and 15kg bags available from the yard.

Doncaster is built on hills, and that shapes every garden in it. The 1960s and 1970s brick homes around Ruffey Lake Park and Doncaster Road sit on sloping blocks with retaining walls, split levels and steep driveways, and a large share of them are being replaced by substantial new builds. Those new houses usually come with a full landscaping plan: paved entertaining areas, synthetic or instant lawn, raised beds and a lot of concrete.

Hard landscaping leads the orders here. Crushed rock goes under driveways and paths, and crusher dust is the compacted base for the Smart Grass that suits the small, terraced lawns these houses end up with. Our bulk concrete mix covers the fence posts, steps and small slabs that a sloping block generates, with cement, reo and bar chairs in the shop. Once the planting goes in, black midnight mulch is the finish most Doncaster builds ask for against dark render and timber battens.

Delivering on a slope

Bulk loads go out by the cubic metre and are tipped where the site has room. On a steep Doncaster driveway that needs a bit of care: loose material will roll, so the driver may suggest tipping at the top of the drive or on a flat part of the nature strip. Coloured mulch should go on a tarp if it is landing on exposed aggregate or pavers. If the site cannot take a truck, we can load a trailer or ute at the yard, or you can take as many 15kg bags as the job needs.

The ground in Doncaster is heavy clay with a lot of fill on the cut-and-fill blocks, and it drains badly on the low side of a slope. Scoria and ag pipe for a drain behind a retaining wall are worth adding to the order if the wall is going in at the same time.

Call (03) 9890 3901 with the Doncaster address and what stage the job is at, and we will line up the delivery to suit the trades on site.

Delivery to Doncaster

How long does delivery to Doncaster take?

Doncaster is about fifteen minutes from the Surrey Hills yard. We deliver across Melbourne's east in less than two hours and Doncaster runs are usually well within that.

My driveway is steep. Where will the load be tipped?

The driver will pick the safest flat spot, usually the top of the driveway or the nature strip. Mention the slope when you order so we can plan the tip.
