Ringwood is where the suburbs start to open out. Blocks are bigger than closer in, the ground rises and falls toward the Dandenongs, and the streets around Ringwood Lake Park and out toward Ringwood North are full of tall gums and native understorey. The houses are mostly brick from the 1960s onward, with a growing cluster of apartments and townhouses near Eastland and the station, but the typical Ringwood garden is still a large, treed block with lawn, sheds, a long driveway and plenty of edges to maintain.

Large blocks mean large volumes. 20mm pine bark is the mulch most Ringwood customers order because it spreads further per cubic metre and blends with the gum litter that falls on everything. Native planting is the norm this far east, so native garden mix is a steady order for new beds. Long gravel driveways and paths are common, and crushed rock for the base with Tuscan toppings on top is the usual combination. For retaining walls on the sloping blocks, large rubble rock can be ordered in for bigger jobs.

Why order from Surrey Hills?

Ringwood has local suppliers, so people sometimes ask why they would ring a yard twenty-odd minutes away. The honest answer is product and timing: if we have what you want in the bay and the truck can be there in under two hours, the distance does not matter much. Bulk loads are delivered by the cubic metre and tipped on the driveway or nature strip, and because the run is longer we try to group Ringwood deliveries so the truck arrives when you need it rather than whenever it is passing.

If you are coming to the yard, a trailer load is a reasonable trip down Canterbury Road, and 15kg bags are there for small jobs. For anything over a trailer load, though, the delivery is worth it. Ringwood's clay is heavier and stonier than closer to town, so loosening it and adding organic matter before planting is worth the effort.

Delivery to Ringwood

Is Ringwood within your under-2-hour delivery zone?

Ringwood is about twenty to twenty-five minutes from the yard and we deliver bulk loads there. The under-2-hour promise applies across Melbourne's east; ring and we will give you a realistic arrival time for the day.

Should I drive to Surrey Hills with a trailer or get a Ringwood delivery?

For one trailer load the trip is reasonable. For anything larger, a bulk delivery by the cubic metre is less work and saves repeat trips. Call for current minimums and delivery fees.

Can you supply large rock for a retaining wall in Ringwood?

Yes. Large rubble rock is ordered in on request with a minimum of 6 cubic metres and delivered in bulk. Ring to discuss the size you need.
