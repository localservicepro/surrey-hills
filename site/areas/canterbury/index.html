Garden Supplies Delivered to Canterbury

Canterbury sits right next door to the yard, a few minutes west along Canterbury Road. We deliver bulk mulch, soil, sand and stone to Canterbury by the cubic metre, and anyone with a trailer can be loaded and home in a quarter of an hour.

Canterbury's streets are some of the most established in Melbourne, with Victorian and Edwardian houses on wide, tree-lined blocks around Maling Road and the Golden Mile along Mont Albert Road. The gardens match the houses: formal front lawns, clipped hedges, rose beds, and a lot of old brick paths, edging and retaining walls that need patching from time to time. Many of these properties have been in the same hands for decades and the owners know exactly what they want.

The product list reflects that. 10mm pine bark is the mulch of choice for beds near the house because it sits flat and looks tidy against a heritage facade, while the bigger beds under old oaks and elms take 20mm pine bark that spreads further and needs topping up less often. Vegie gardens and new plantings go into premium organic soil. For the gravel paths and driveways that suit these houses, Tuscan toppings give a warm, period-appropriate finish, and we keep white brick sand for anyone repointing a garden wall or relaying a brick path.

Delivery to Canterbury

With the yard this close, delivery is simple. Bulk loads go out by the cubic metre and are tipped on the driveway or nature strip, and because it is such a short run we can often slot a Canterbury drop between bigger deliveries further out. If you only need a bit, 15kg bags from the yard are quick to collect, and a BYO trailer gets loaded on the spot. The streets around Canterbury are wide, so truck access is rarely an issue, but let us know if there is a low overhanging tree or a tight crossover.

One thing to keep in mind: the soil around here is Melbourne's usual heavy clay once you are past the topsoil, and some of the older beds have become hydrophobic after years of mulching with fine material. Forking through organic mulch or adding washed sand to the soil before planting helps with both problems. Ask when you ring and we will suggest the right mix.

Ring the yard on (03) 9890 3901 with your address and the number of metres, or drop in on Canterbury Road to see the products before you order.
