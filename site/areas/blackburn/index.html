Garden Supplies Delivered to Blackburn

Blackburn is about ten to twelve minutes east of the yard along Canterbury Road. We deliver bulk mulch, soil, sand and stone to Blackburn, Blackburn North and Blackburn South by the cubic metre, and the drive is short enough for a trailer run.

Blackburn is the greenest suburb we deliver to. The streets around Blackburn Lake Sanctuary and the Creeklands are under a canopy of eucalypts, and the council's tree protection rules mean that canopy is not going anywhere. Blocks here tend to be large and often sloping, with houses from the 1950s to the 1970s set back among the trees and gardens that lean towards natives and bush-style planting rather than formal lawns and hedges.

That changes what people order. Native garden mix is far more popular in Blackburn than almost anywhere else we deliver, because it drains freely and keeps the nutrient levels low the way grevilleas, banksias and correas like. Under the gums, 20mm pine bark is the mulch of choice since bark and leaf litter disappear into it. Where a bed has become tired, organic mulch forked through the clay puts organic matter back without overdoing the nutrients.

Sloping ground and clay also mean water problems. Plenty of Blackburn houses get a wet side yard or a damp slab in winter, and the fix is usually an ag drain down the high side. We supply the scoria to backfill the trench and the ag pipe, pits and geotextile from drainage supplies to go with it. A metre of scoria goes a long way in a 300mm trench, so tell us the trench length and we will work out the volume.

Getting the load in among the trees

Delivery to Blackburn is straightforward on most streets, but the tree canopy can be low over a driveway. When you ring, tell us if there are overhanging branches or a narrow crossover and the driver will tip on the nature strip instead. Bulk loads go out by the cubic metre; smaller jobs are covered by 15kg bags, and if you have a trailer or ute we will load it at the yard.

Delivery to Blackburn

How long does a delivery to Blackburn take?

Blackburn is about ten to twelve minutes from the yard. We deliver across Melbourne's east in less than two hours, and a Blackburn load is normally well inside that.

My driveway has low branches. Can the truck still get in?

Tell us when you order. If the driveway is not clear the driver will tip on the nature strip or another spot you nominate. Alternatively bring a trailer to the yard and we will load it.

Is there a minimum for scoria or native mix delivered to Blackburn?

Call for current minimums and delivery fees. Small drainage jobs can be done with 15kg bags of scoria picked up from the yard.
