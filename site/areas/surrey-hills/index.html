Surrey Hills is where we are, and it is the suburb we know best. The streets between Canterbury Road and Riversdale Road are a mix of Federation and interwar brick homes on generous blocks, a lot of them with deep back gardens that have been planted and replanted over the better part of a century. Mature elms, liquidambars and plane trees line many of the streets, so there is plenty of shade, plenty of leaf drop, and the usual heavy clay underneath once you dig past the topsoil.

That combination shapes what locals order. Established beds under big trees get topped up with 10mm pine bark because fallen leaves blend into it and it does not blow around in a northerly. Tired beds that have been worked for decades get a few cubic metres of organic mulch forked through to put some life back into the clay. When someone pulls out an old lawn or builds a raised bed, blended soil is the usual call, and for a new path or shed base we load crushed rock and washed sand onto the same truck.

Ordering from a yard that is already in the suburb

Being local changes how people buy. A lot of Surrey Hills customers simply drive down Canterbury Road with the trailer on, get loaded, and are back home spreading mulch twenty minutes later. Others grab a few 15kg bags on the way past for a planter box or a patch of lawn repair. For anything over a trailer load it makes more sense to have us deliver it in bulk by the cubic metre; the truck tips it where you want it, on the nature strip or in the driveway, and you are not shovelling it out of a trailer twice.

Because the run from the yard to anywhere in Surrey Hills is only a few minutes, we can usually fit a local delivery in around the longer runs out to the east. Tell us roughly where on the block you want the load tipped, keep the spot clear of cars, and put a tarp down if you are ordering coloured mulch near pavers.

Not sure how many metres you need? Measure the bed, tell us the depth you are after, and we will do the sums for you. Call the yard on (03) 9890 3901, or come in and have a look at the bays before you decide.
