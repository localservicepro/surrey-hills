Garden Supplies Delivered to Kew

Kew is about fifteen minutes north-west of the yard, up through Balwyn and along Cotham Road or Barkers Road. We deliver bulk mulch, soil, sand and stone to Kew and Kew East by the cubic metre, and plenty of Kew landscapers collect from the yard.

Kew is a suburb of big houses on big, often steep blocks. Around Studley Park and the streets dropping toward the Yarra, the land falls away sharply and the gardens are terraced with retaining walls, steps and long runs of garden bed. Closer to Kew Junction and High Street the blocks are flatter but still generous, with mature gardens that have been professionally maintained for decades. Either way, the volumes involved are bigger than in most suburbs we deliver to.

Large beds on slopes need a mulch that stays put, which is why 20mm pine bark is the default for Kew. It is heavy enough not to wash down the hill in a storm and it spreads further than the fine grade. Retaining walls need drainage behind them, so scoria goes out by the metre along with ag pipe from the drainage range. New terraces and paths sit on a base of crushed rock, and the planting that follows goes into blended soil, or native garden mix for the bush-style gardens that are common on the Yarra side.

Landscapers and large orders

A lot of Kew work is done by landscape contractors rather than owners, and they order in bulk. Loads are delivered by the cubic metre and tipped on site, usually on the driveway since the steeper streets have narrow nature strips. On a big job we can stagger deliveries across the day so the crew is not working around a mountain of material. For a homeowner topping up a few beds, 15kg bags or a loaded trailer from the yard are the simpler route.

Steep driveways are worth mentioning when you order. A loaded truck tipping on a slope needs a bit of thought about where the material will end up, and the driver can advise on the day. Kew's soil is clay, and on the terraced blocks it has often been cut and filled, so adding organic matter before planting is worthwhile.

Ring the yard on (03) 9890 3901 with the Kew address, the number of metres and a note about the driveway, and we will get it on the truck.

Delivery to Kew

How long does a bulk delivery to Kew take?

Kew is about fifteen minutes from the yard. We deliver across Melbourne's east in less than two hours, and Kew loads normally arrive comfortably inside that.

Can you deliver to a steep driveway in Kew?

Usually. Tell us when you order and the driver will choose the safest spot to tip. If the driveway is too steep, the nature strip or a flat section of the street is the alternative.
