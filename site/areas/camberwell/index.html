Garden Supplies Delivered to Camberwell

Camberwell is about ten minutes from our yard, down Canterbury Road and across to Burke Road. We deliver mulch, soil, sand and stone to Camberwell in bulk by the cubic metre, and the yard is close enough that a trailer run is an easy morning job.

Most of Camberwell was built between the wars, and the blocks are big. Walk the streets off Riversdale Road or Prospect Hill Road and you see solid brick and tile homes with wide front lawns, hedges along the footpath and long backyards that often slope away toward Gardiners Creek. Plenty of those backyards have been reworked with a pool, a deck or a second storey over the years, but the basic layout of lawn, beds and big trees is the same.

Big beds under big trees want a chunky mulch, so 20mm pine bark is what most Camberwell gardeners order by the metre. Lawns get re-laid often enough that instant turf and the blended soil that goes underneath it move together. Where a block slopes, water pools against the house in winter, which is why scoria for agricultural drains is on a lot of Camberwell orders, along with crushed rock for the paths and driveway bases that come with a renovation.

Trade and home gardeners

Camberwell keeps a lot of landscapers and builders busy, and we supply both the trade and the owner doing it themselves. Tradespeople tend to order bulk by the cubic metre and have it tipped on site first thing; the under-2-hour delivery means a crew is not standing around waiting for soil. Homeowners are more likely to come to the yard with a trailer, or pick up a dozen 15kg bags for a weekend job. Either way the products come out of the same bays.

The clay in Camberwell is heavy and sets like concrete in summer, so if you are planting into it, mix organic matter or washed sand through before you add new soil on top. Tell us what you are planting and we can point you to the right blend. Gypsum and other soil conditioners are on the shelf in the shop.

When you are ready, ring (03) 9890 3901 with the address, how many metres, and where on the property you want it tipped. If the driveway is steep or the crossover is tight, mention it so the driver knows what to expect.

Delivery to Camberwell

How long will delivery to Camberwell take?

Camberwell is about ten minutes from the Surrey Hills yard. We deliver across Melbourne's east in less than two hours, and a Camberwell run is normally well inside that window.

Is it cheaper to bring a trailer or have it delivered to Camberwell?

A trailer load picked up from the yard avoids the delivery run, but once you are past a trailer load, bulk delivery by the cubic metre is the practical choice. Call for current minimums and delivery fees.

Can you deliver turf and the soil for it on the same day?

Yes. Order the blended soil first so it can be spread and levelled, then the instant turf. Turf is ordered in fresh, so ring ahead to line up both deliveries.
