Garden Supplies Delivered to Nunawading

Nunawading is about fifteen minutes east of the yard along Canterbury Road or Whitehorse Road. We deliver bulk garden and building supplies to Nunawading by the cubic metre, and the trip to Surrey Hills with a trailer is an easy one for anyone on the Whitehorse Road side.

Nunawading is half residential and half working suburb. South of the railway line the streets are 1960s brick veneers on good-sized blocks, many of them now split into two or three units, while along Whitehorse Road and around the industrial pockets there are workshops, showrooms and small factories with yards and car parks of their own. We supply both sides: the family in a unit tidying a courtyard and the business owner who needs a car park base or a drainage trench backfilled.

On the trade side, crushed rock is the big mover, used under hardstands, driveways and slabs, with washed sand and bulk concrete mix for the concrete that follows. Reo, mesh, bar chairs and builders plastic are in the shop. On the residential side, unit developments finish their front setbacks with 20mm pine bark because it covers a lot of ground per metre, and blended soil goes in wherever the builder has left bare subsoil behind.

Delivering to units and businesses

Unit developments in Nunawading usually share a central driveway, and the question is always where the load can be tipped without blocking the neighbours. A front nature strip is the usual answer; if there is not one, a loaded trailer from the yard or 15kg bags are the fallback. Businesses are simpler: tell us the gate, the hours someone is on site and where the material goes, and the truck will tip it there. Bulk loads are sold by the cubic metre, and bigger orders can be split across the day.

The ground through Nunawading is the same heavy clay found across the east, and on the flat blocks it holds water. If you are putting down a slab or a shed base, compacting crusher dust or crushed rock over the clay first will save trouble later. For garden beds, work organic matter in before adding soil on top.
