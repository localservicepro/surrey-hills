Garden Supplies Delivered to Burwood

Burwood is about ten minutes south-east of the yard, straight down Warrigal Road or Elgar Road. We deliver bulk garden supplies to Burwood and Burwood East by the cubic metre, and Burwood customers with a trailer are a regular sight at the yard on weekends.

Burwood has a bit of everything. Around Deakin University and the Burwood Highway tram line, the old brick veneers on quarter-acre blocks are being replaced by rows of townhouses, each with a small courtyard and a strip of front garden to finish. Further toward Wattle Park and the Surrey Hills border, the original houses and their big gardens are still in place, with the lawns, vegie beds and established trees that go with them.

Townhouse courtyards are mostly about getting a usable surface down fast. For a real lawn that means blended soil levelled 100mm deep with instant turf rolled over it; for a lawn that needs no mowing it means a compacted crusher dust base with Smart Grass on top. Paved courtyards need washed sand for the screed bed. The bigger older gardens order pine bark and organic mulch by the metre the way the rest of the east does.

Rental properties and student housing

With the university on the doorstep, a lot of Burwood houses are rentals, and landlords and property managers ring us to tidy a garden between tenants. That usually means a few cubic metres of mulch to cover beds that have been let go, some blended soil to patch a lawn, and a bag of lawn seed. These jobs are often time-sensitive, so the under-2-hour delivery suits them well.

Access varies a lot. Older houses have a driveway and a wide nature strip, and a bulk load can be tipped straight onto either. Townhouse developments frequently share a single driveway, and the body corporate may want notice before a truck tips on common property. If that is your situation, 15kg bags from the yard or a loaded trailer are the easier path. Tell us when you ring and we will suggest which way to go.

Call the yard on (03) 9890 3901 with the Burwood address and what you need, and we will give you a delivery time for the day.

Delivery to Burwood

How long does delivery to Burwood take?

Burwood is around ten minutes from the yard. Our delivery promise across Melbourne's east is less than two hours, and Burwood loads are normally well within that.

Do I need a trailer, or will you deliver a small amount to Burwood?

We deliver bulk by the cubic metre. For small amounts, 15kg bags from the yard or a BYO trailer are the better options. Call for current minimums and delivery fees.

Can you deliver turf and soil to a Burwood rental between tenants?

Yes. Order the blended soil first, spread and level it, then the instant turf, which is ordered in fresh. Ring ahead so both land when you need them.
