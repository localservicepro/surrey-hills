Garden Supplies Delivered to Box Hill

Box Hill is a short run east along Canterbury Road from our yard, about five to ten minutes depending on the lights at Elgar Road. We deliver bulk loads by the cubic metre to Box Hill, Box Hill North and Box Hill South, and plenty of Box Hill customers pick up bags or bring a trailer.

Few suburbs in Melbourne's east have changed as much as Box Hill over the last fifteen years. The streets around Box Hill Central are now apartment towers, while the blocks further out in Box Hill North and Box Hill South are being split, with a 1950s brick veneer coming down and two or three townhouses going up in its place. Each of those townhouses has a small courtyard, a strip of side garden and a front setback that needs finishing, and that is the kind of job we supply every week.

Small outdoor spaces push people towards low-maintenance finishes. Synthetic lawn is popular in Box Hill courtyards, which means a compacted base of crusher dust, a roll of Smart Grass and a few bags of kiln dried sand to dress the pile. For the planted strips along the fence, black midnight mulch suits the dark render and timber cladding most of these builds use. Where a builder has left nothing but subsoil behind, a metre or two of blended soil gets a garden started.

The older part of Box Hill is a different job. Interwar homes near Box Hill Gardens still sit on full-size blocks with lawn out the front and a vegie patch and fruit trees behind, and those gardens take the same mix of mulch, soil and sand that the rest of the east does. The clay is heavy here too, so gypsum from the garden products shelf often goes on the truck with the soil.

Getting it to a tight site

Access is the thing to think about in Box Hill. New townhouse developments often share a narrow driveway, and apartment buildings have nowhere to tip a bulk load at all. If that is your situation, 15kg bags from the yard are the practical answer, and a ute or trailer can be loaded at the yard with whatever you need. Where there is a driveway or a clear nature strip, bulk delivery by the cubic metre is cheaper per metre and saves you the trip.

Tell us the address and where you want the load tipped when you ring, and mention if the street has parking restrictions or a body corporate that needs notice. Call (03) 9890 3901 to organise a Box Hill delivery or to check what is in the bays today.

Delivery to Box Hill

How long does delivery to Box Hill take?

Box Hill is one of the closest suburbs to the yard, about five to ten minutes along Canterbury Road. We deliver in less than two hours across Melbourne's east, and Box Hill runs are usually well inside that.

I live in a townhouse with a shared driveway. Can you still deliver?

Usually, as long as there is a spot the truck can tip onto without blocking neighbours. If there is not, we can load your trailer or ute at the yard, or you can take 15kg bags.
