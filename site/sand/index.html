Sand in Surrey Hills

Sand for trade and home gardeners

Sand sounds simple until you use the wrong one. Brick sand holds together in mortar, washed sand drains under pavers, triple washed sand is clean enough for tiling and rendering, and kiln dried sand is bone dry so it flows into the joints of pavers and the pile of synthetic grass. Surrey Hills Garden Supplies keeps all four on the yard on Canterbury Road, plus packing sand for base work. Sand is sold loose by the cubic metre for trailers and truck deliveries, bagged for pickup, and kiln dried sand is also available in 20kg bags and one-tonne bulker bags. Bricklayers, pavers, tilers, landscapers and home renovators all buy from the same bays, and we are happy to talk you through which grade suits the job. In Melbourne's eastern suburbs we aim to have bulk sand on site in less than two hours.

How to choose

Which sand product do I need?

Bricklaying and mortar

White brick sand, also called builders sand. Fine, with a little clay, so mortar is workable and holds its shape on the trowel.

Paving base and concrete

Washed sand. Coarse and clean, so it compacts well under pavers and is the standard sand for site-mixed concrete.

Tiling, rendering and bedding

Triple washed sand. Washed three times to remove clay and silt, which keeps tile adhesives and renders clean and strong.

Synthetic grass infill and paver joints

Kiln dried sand. Oven dried so it pours freely, in 20kg bags, one-tonne bulker bags or bulk loads up to 12 cubic metres.

Good to know

Sand questions

What is the difference between washed sand and brick sand?

Brick sand is finer and contains a small amount of clay so mortar sticks and stays workable. Washed sand is coarser with the clay washed out, so it drains and compacts, which is what you want under pavers and in concrete.

How much sand fits in a 6x4 trailer?

About half a cubic metre, which weighs around 750kg. Check your trailer's rated capacity before loading.

Do you sell sand in bulker bags?

Kiln dried sand is available in one-tonne bulker bags. Ask about bulker bags for other sands when you call.

Which sand do I use to top dress artificial grass?

Kiln dried silica sand. It is dry and free flowing so it works down between the blades and weighs the grass down. Our blog post on kiln dried sand applications explains the method.
