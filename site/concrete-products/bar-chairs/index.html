Bar Chairs

Four sizes of bar chair to hold mesh and reo at the correct height in a slab or footing.

Bar chairs are the small plastic supports that hold reinforcing mesh and bar off the ground so the steel ends up in the middle of the concrete, not the bottom. Surrey Hills Garden Supplies carries four sizes: 25/40mm, 50/65mm and 75/90mm chairs for slab mesh, and trench bar chairs for 8–12mm steel in footings. They are sold by the bag at the yard alongside ute mesh, trench mesh and reo bar, and can be added to any delivery.

Good to know

Bar Chairs questions

Which bar chair height do I need?

Match the chair to the concrete cover your slab needs. For a 100mm residential slab with mesh in the middle, 50/65 chairs are common. Trench chairs suit footings.

How many bar chairs per sheet of mesh?

Allow roughly one chair per square metre, more at sheet edges and laps, so the mesh cannot sag under foot traffic during the pour.
