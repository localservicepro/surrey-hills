Additives & Oxides

Oxides, sealers, bonding agents, waterproofers, plasticisers, retarders and acids for concrete and masonry.

The additives shelf covers the chemistry that goes with concrete and masonry: Colorlinx oxides in six colours to tint concrete and mortar, Auseal acrylic sealers and xylene thinner, Gibco MRF admixture, Best Bond bonding agent, brickies red waterproofer, mortar plasticiser, cold bitumen, hydrochloric acid for cleaning, exposed aggregate retarder and liquid lime for lawns and soil. All are available at the Surrey Hills yard and can be added to a delivery.

Auseal Sealers & Solvents

AUSEAL – EC25 is a general purpose concrete sealant specifically formulated for sealing and protecting dry concrete based domestic floors and driveways where a medium gloss finish is required. AUSEAL EC25 is based on 100% acrylic copolymer.

AUSEAL BLEND XYLENE is used as a thinner for Auseal – Curite solvent based sealer, and also as a general cleaner for machinery and applicators.

Auseal Blend Solvent can also be used to clean concrete sealers and prepare them for sealing. It can also soak out grease and oil stains from concrete prior to sealing.

  • Appearance: Clear colourless low – viscous liquid
  • Total Solids: N/A
  • pH: N/A
  • Specific Gravity: 0,8
  • Water Solubility: Immiscible
  • Min Film Forming Temp: N/A
  • Min Film Thickness: N/A
  • Application rate: See Below.

Auseal Blend Solvent is used for thinning down solvent based resin products, either because of high viscosity, or to assist penetration into the substrate. An exception is 50% solvent for Hardseal, High Gloss or Supreme Seal is recommended.

Auseal Blend Solvent is used for cleaning spray equipment, broom heads or rollers. If using to clean spraying machines just flush with solvent.

  • DO NOT use on plastics or surfaces affected by solvents.
  • DO NOT use on paint, lacquered, varnished or polished surfaces.
  • DO NOT use on carpets, vinyls or similar floor surfaces.
  • DO NOT use on any glazed products.
  • Contains flammable solvents and precautions should be taken when using.

Auseal Blend Solvent is available in 1, 5, 10, 20 and 200 litre drums.

Refer to the Material Safety Data Sheet before handling or use.

Gibco MRF Admixture

A new product line carried at SHGS. Gibco MFR produces superior mortars at substantial savings in time and expense. MRF mortars are high strength, virtually waterproof and colorfast without disbonding. MRF chemical concentrate solves the problems of excess tempering, efflorescence, freeze-thaw breakup, water penetration and lime burns. MRF mortars meet or exceed design specifications.

Typical applications are for: Mortar and rendering. SHGS carries the 1 Ltr and 5 Ltr sizes. Recommended dosage is 40ml per 20kg of Cement.

Best Bond

SHGG Carries in stock Best bond in 4 sizes. 1 Ltr, 2 Ltr, 4Ltr and 20 Ltr. Its Suitable for many uses, which include: Cement toppings, rendering, sealing, plaster/hard plaster, dust proofing, binding, bonding new to old concrete, woodwork and carpentry adhesive. Note: This product is not suitable to be used in permanently wet areas.

Lime Juice

LIME JUICE is a colourless liquid admixture, engineered as a lime alternative in lime-cement mortars. Lime Juice provides all the technical features of traditional lime compounds without the hazard of being highly caustic.

Lime Juice complies with the Australian Masonry Structures AS 3700 Standards. Developed by brickies, for brickies, it is the safer alternative that’s cheaper and easier to use. With one five-litre bottle equal to 10 x 20-kilogram bags of lime, it is the cost-saver you need on your job site, significantly reducing the cost of mortar. Available at Surrey Hills Garden Supplies in a 5 litre or 500 ml containers.

Liquid Lime

Gibco’s LIQUID LIME plasticizing characteristics allow it to replace lime, clay, and other plasticizers entirely, and lend hand to substantial savings with 5 Litres replacing up to 45 Bags (900KG) of lime. Whether you are working with Block, Brick, or Stone, MRF gets the whole job done at a fraction of the cost of traditional 20kg bags of Lime. Available at Surrey Hills Garden Supplies in 5 Litre, 1 Litre and 500 ml sizes.

Additives & Oxides questions

How much oxide do I add to concrete?

Oxide is dosed as a percentage of the cement weight and the result varies with the sand, cement and water used. Follow the pack rate and mix a test batch first.
