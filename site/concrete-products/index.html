Concrete Products in Surrey Hills

Concrete Products for trade and home gardeners

Surrey Hills Garden Supplies carries the Cement Australia bagged range so concreters, bricklayers and home builders in Melbourne's east can get everything for a pour in one stop: general purpose cement, builders cement, off-white cement, concrete mix, rapid set, mortar mix and hydrated lime, all in 20kg bags. Alongside the bags we stock reinforcing steel (ute mesh, trench mesh, reo bar and starter bars), bar chairs in four heights, builders plastic and dampcourse, and additives such as oxides, waterproofers, plasticisers and sealers. Bulk concrete mix is also available by the cubic metre for larger jobs. Everything can be picked up from the Canterbury Road yard or delivered on our trucks, and in the eastern suburbs we aim for delivery in less than two hours. Trade accounts and the public are both welcome. If you are unsure which cement or mesh a job needs, call and talk to the yard before you order.

How to choose

Which concrete products product do I need?

Footings, slabs and general concreting

General purpose cement mixed with washed sand and screenings, or bagged concrete mix for small jobs such as mower strips and post footings.

Fence posts and clothes hoists

Rapid set. Pour it dry into the hole, add water and it hardens in minutes.

Bricklaying and blockwork

Builders cement or mortar mix with white brick sand, plus hydrated lime for workability.

Reinforcing a slab or footing

Ute mesh for small slabs, trench mesh for strip footings, and bar chairs to hold the steel at the right height.

Good to know

Concrete Products questions

How many 20kg bags of concrete mix make a cubic metre?

Roughly 110 bags of 20kg concrete mix make one cubic metre. For anything over a few hundred litres, bulk concrete mix delivered by the metre is far cheaper and quicker.

Do you deliver bagged cement?

Yes. Bagged cement products can be purchased at the yard or delivered at an additional cost, on their own or with a bulk load of sand or rock.

What is the difference between GP cement and builders cement?

General purpose cement is straight cement for all-round concrete work. Builders cement is blended with fly ash or slag for better workability and later-age strength in mortars, grouts and general concrete.

Do you stock reinforcing mesh and bar?

Yes. Ute mesh, trench mesh, reo bar and starter bars are listed on the steel page, with bar chairs to suit.

Can I colour concrete?

Yes. We stock oxides in a range of colours, plus other additives, on the additives page.
