Rock & Stone in Surrey Hills

Rock & Stone for trade and home gardeners

Rock and stone do the structural work in a landscape: the base under a driveway or shed, the drainage layer behind a retaining wall, the bedding for pipes and the decorative finish on paths and garden beds. Surrey Hills Garden Supplies stocks the grades Melbourne landscapers, plumbers and builders ask for most: 20mm crushed rock, 14mm screenings, 7mm (quarter minus) aggregate, crusher dust, scoria, Tuscan toppings and larger rubble rock from 40mm to 100mm. Everything is held in bays on our Canterbury Road yard and can be bagged into 15kg bags, loaded into your trailer, or delivered by the cubic metre on our trucks. Rock is heavy, so for anything more than a few bags a delivery is usually the easiest option. We aim to deliver in the eastern suburbs in less than two hours. The calculator on each product page converts length, width and depth into cubic metres and tonnes.

How to choose

Which rock & stone product do I need?

Driveways, shed bases and compacted fill

20mm crushed rock with fines. It locks together when compacted and is the standard base material.

Concrete and drainage pipe bedding

14mm screenings. Clean, single-size stone used in concrete and around agricultural pipe.

Paving and under slabs

7mm aggregate (quarter minus) or crusher dust. Both compact to a smooth, firm bed for pavers.

Drainage behind walls and in trenches

Scoria. Light, porous volcanic rock that plumbers and landscapers use for free-draining backfill.

Paths and decorative beds

Tuscan toppings for a warm, compacted gravel path, or large rubble rock for dry creek beds and feature areas.

Good to know

Rock & Stone questions

How much does crushed rock weigh?

About 1.6 tonnes per cubic metre. Scoria is much lighter at roughly 0.9 tonnes per cubic metre, which is one reason it is used behind retaining walls.

What is the difference between crushed rock and screenings?

Crushed rock includes fines (dust) so it compacts into a solid base. Screenings are washed, single-size stone with no fines, so water passes straight through.

Can I bring my own trailer for rock?

Yes. Back up to the bay and we will load it. Remember rock is heavy: a 6x4 trailer takes about half a cubic metre, which is around 800kg of crushed rock.

Do you sell rock in bags?

Yes. Every rock product can be bagged into 15kg bags at the yard for small jobs and top-ups.

How deep should a crushed rock base be?

For a garden path 75mm compacted is typical; for a driveway allow 100–150mm compacted in layers. Use the calculator and add 10% for compaction.
