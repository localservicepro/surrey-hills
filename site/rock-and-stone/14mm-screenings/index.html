14mm Screenings

Clean 14mm single-size stone for concrete, drainage and pipe bedding.

Screenings are the aggregate in concrete. Our 14mm screenings are a screened, single-size bluestone with no fines, so they also make an ideal free-draining backfill behind walls, bedding around agricultural pipe and a hard-wearing topping. Mixed with washed sand and cement they produce a standard concrete. Bagged into 15kg bags, loaded into trailers or delivered in bulk from Surrey Hills to Melbourne's east.

About 14mm screenings

Screenings are the preferred stone used in concrete production. it’s a screened Granular product and has an average particle size of 14mm. Ideally used for a range of construction and landscape projects.

  • Stable base for a range of construction and landscape projects.
  • Can be used with Washed sand and cement to produce Concrete.
  • Can be used as a backfill /drainage product
  • Can be used as toppings.
  • Screenings are a natural product so slight variations can occur.

Surrey Hills Garden Supplies can bag the product into 15kg bags, and can supply bulk loads, truck loads or BYO your trailer to the site.

14mm Screenings questions

What is the concrete mix ratio with screenings?

A common site mix is 1 part cement, 2 parts washed sand and 3 parts 14mm screenings by volume. For small jobs, bagged concrete mix is easier.
