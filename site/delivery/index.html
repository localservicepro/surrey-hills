Delivery from Surrey Hills

Deliveries in less than 2 hours across the eastern suburbs

Bulk loads by the cubic metre on our tipper trucks, bagged product for pickup, or your own trailer loaded at the yard.

How delivery works

Call (03) 9890 3901 or send a quote request with the product, the quantity in cubic metres (use the calculator) and the delivery address. We confirm the price and a delivery window on the phone. In Melbourne's eastern suburbs we aim to have a bulk load on site in less than two hours. Loads are tipped where the truck can safely reach: a driveway, nature strip or the kerb. Tell us if access is tight, if there are overhead wires, or if you want the load on a tarp.

Pickup and BYO trailer

The yard is at {{STREET_NUMBER}} Canterbury Road, Surrey Hills VIC 3127. Bulk products are bagged into 15kg bags for pickup (20kg for kiln dried sand). If you bring a trailer, back up to the bay and we load it: a standard 6x4 trailer carries about half a cubic metre, which is roughly 600kg of soil or 800kg of crushed rock, so check your trailer's rated capacity. Bring a tarp or cover for the trip home.
