20mm Pine Bark

Chunky brown pine bark for larger beds, slopes and under trees. Long lasting and low maintenance.

20mm pine bark is the bigger brother of our 10mm bark: the same plantation pine, screened to a chunkier grade that spreads further and lasts longer between top-ups. It is the mulch to choose for big open beds, slopes and the areas under established trees where leaves fall and need to blend in. Available in 15kg bags, by the trailer load or delivered in bulk from the Surrey Hills yard.

About 20mm pine bark

20mm Pine Bark is a larger version of the Pine Bark and brown in appearance. 20mm Pine is generally used to cover larger open areas or larger garden beds. It’s also known as Tan Bark. It is renowned for its ability not to blow or wash away, but still allows air flow to the soil, as well as being excellent at water retention. It is long lasting, has good capacity to absorb and retain moisture, and is also extremely low maintenance. Installation should be to a depth of 100mm for new applications and typically 50-75mm for “top up” installations.

20mm pine mulch is great to spread under trees and shrubs where leaves and bark fall often as it will blend into the mulch. Pine Bark does not breakdown quickly. Due to its chunky appearance, it generally spreads further making it suitable for larger garden beds. It should be applied to the soil surface and not dug into the soil. It’s important to keep mulches away from the stems of plants to prevent mould or fungal disease in the bark

20mm Pine Bark questions

Should I use 10mm or 20mm pine bark?

Use 10mm for a fine finish on beds close to the house, and 20mm for large beds, slopes and under trees where a chunkier, longer-lasting cover suits better.
