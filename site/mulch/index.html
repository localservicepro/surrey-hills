Mulch in Surrey Hills

Mulch for trade and home gardeners

Mulch is the quickest way to make a garden bed look finished and to keep it that way. A layer spread over the soil holds moisture in through a Melbourne summer, keeps roots cool, suppresses weeds and slowly feeds the soil as it breaks down. Surrey Hills Garden Supplies keeps a full range of mulch on the yard on Canterbury Road: 10mm and 20mm pine bark, black midnight mulch, organic mulch and certified soft-play mulch for playgrounds. Every product can be bagged into 15kg bags for pickup, loaded into your own trailer, or delivered in bulk on one of our trucks. We service both trades and the general public, and in the eastern suburbs we aim to deliver in less than two hours. If you are not sure how much you need, use the volume calculator on each product page: as a rule, one cubic metre covers about 10 square metres at 100mm deep.

How to choose

Which mulch product do I need?

Garden beds you want to look sharp

10mm pine bark gives a fine, even, chocolate-brown finish and does not blow or wash away. It is the pick for high-profile beds close to the house.

Big beds, slopes and under trees

20mm pine bark is the same bark in a larger grade. It lasts longer, sits well on slopes and hides fallen leaves and bark.

A dark, contemporary look

Black midnight mulch is a dyed, recycled timber mulch that holds its colour and makes greens and flower colour stand out.

Feeding the soil

Organic mulch is composted and breaks down faster, adding organic matter. Good for vegie patches and anywhere you want to improve the soil.

Playgrounds and fall zones

Soft play mulch is a certified playground grade. It needs to be installed to the right depth, so check the product page and ask us before ordering.

Good to know

Mulch questions

How deep should I lay mulch?

Lay mulch 75–100mm deep on a new bed and 50–75mm when topping up. Keep it clear of plant stems and tree trunks to avoid rot and fungal problems in the bark.

How much mulch do I need?

One cubic metre of mulch covers roughly 10 square metres at 100mm deep, or about 13 square metres at 75mm. Use the calculator on any mulch page and add 10% for settling.

Can I get mulch in bags?

Yes. We bag every mulch into 15kg bags for yard pickup. For larger areas a bulk load delivered on our truck, or filling your own trailer, works out far cheaper per metre.

Do you deliver mulch to my suburb?

We deliver bulk mulch across Melbourne's eastern suburbs, usually in less than two hours from Surrey Hills. Call (03) 9890 3901 with your suburb and quantity and we will confirm a time.

Which mulch is best for weed control?

Any mulch laid at 75mm or more will suppress weeds. Pine bark lasts longest between top-ups; organic mulch breaks down faster but improves the soil underneath.
